Test Automation Workshops

Practical, hands-on training in creating maintainable tests for your game. Available for Unreal, Unity, and Godot, covering the tools already available in your engine. Join a public cohort, or bring the workshop in-house for your studio.

{% include elements/testimonial.html text="Even fairly late in the project lifecycle, we were able to hugely improve build health, and are saving hours of dev and tester time every day" name="Daniel Gronner" role="Lead QA, Nosebleed Interactive" logo="assets/nosebleed-logo.png" url="https://www.nosebleedinteractive.com"%}

What You'll Learn

  • The benefits of test automation for game development
  • The fail-fast-fail-hard mindset, and the benefits it brings even without automation
  • The available tooling in your game engine
  • The three most useful test types and when to use them
  • What types of tests to avoid
  • How to structure good tests to make them readable, maintainable and trustworthy

Asset Tests

Looking for configuration problems on-disk and scaling with new data

Smoke Tests

Pragmatically exploring the game from the player's point of view

Actor Tests

Rapidly testing gameplay logic in isolation, giving you fast, actionable feedback

Private Workshops for Studios

Jump-start your team's automation skills with a private workshop, tailored to your project.

  • 1-day crash course — the full syllabus in a single intensive day, in person or remote
  • 3-day masterclass — the syllabus plus hands-on work on your own project, ending with tests that can go live directly

Inside the Cohort

Four weeks of live, trainer-led teaching, with real practice, feedback and a room full of peers between the sessions.

Live Weekly Sessions

Two hours of instructor-led sessions every week, taught live rather than pre-recorded, so you can ask questions as they come up.

Hands-On Demos

Plenty of tests written live in the engine. See the reality of automating real projects, not just theory.

Private Discord

A private server for your cohort: ask questions between sessions, share code, and stay in touch with the group after the course ends.

Resources & Homework

Recordings and materials shared from every session, and exercises so you can practise on real code. Knowledge that sticks with you.

Assessments & Certification

Your homework is assessed and returned with feedback, and you finish the course with a certificate of completion — useful evidence towards professional development goals.
